# No More Space — Game Design

> *The ship is falling into a black hole. Something aboard is not human.
> Find it before there is no more space.*

No More Space is a spooky, slightly silly space version of **Blood on the Clocktower** (BotC). The rules follow BotC's beginner script, *Trouble Brewing*, almost exactly. It's set on a 3D ship you walk around, like **Among Us**, and borrows the paranoia of **The Traitors**.

- **Atmosphere:** eerie. Flickering lights, a black hole that grows outside the windows, creaks and whispers, a heartbeat during votes.
- **Tone:** lighthearted. Hats, pets, rubber-duck deaths and a Captain who makes the crew do the chicken dance.

---

## 1. Teams

| BotC | No More Space | Team | What they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| Townsfolk | **Crew** | Good | Helpful abilities |
| Outsider | **Drifter** | Good | Abilities that make life harder for the good team |
| Minion | **Saboteur** | Evil | Serve the Parasite and spread chaos |
| Demon | **The Parasite** | Evil | Kills each night. If it dies, evil loses |
| Storyteller | **The Captain** (human) or **ARIA** (autopilot) | — | Runs the game |

**Good wins** when the Parasite is dead.
**Evil wins** when only 2 players are left alive. The ship falls into the black hole: no more space.

## 3. Player counts & balance

### Setup table
For 5–15 players this is BotC's own table. The 3–4 rows are our "Short Haul" extension.

| Players | Crew | Drifters | Saboteurs | Parasite | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 1 | Short Haul: the Parasite's first kill is on **night 3** |
| 4 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 1 | Short Haul |
| 5 | 3 | 0 | 1 | 1 | BotC "Teensyville" |
| 6 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | |
| 7 | 5 | 0 | 1 | 1 | Evil know each other from here up |
| 8 | 5 | 1 | 1 | 1 | |
| 9 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 1 | |
| 10 | 7 | 0 | 2 | 1 | |
| 11 | 7 | 1 | 2 | 1 | |
| 12 | 7 | 2 | 2 | 1 | |
| 13 | 9 | 0 | 3 | 1 | |
| 14 | 9 | 1 | 3 | 1 | |
| 15 | 9 | 2 | 3 | 1 | |

### How BotC balances player counts, and what we copy
- **More players means more evil, but also more days.** Evil grows from 2 to 4 players, while each extra player adds roughly half a day to the game.
- **Drifters fill in at the "+1, +2" counts** so a game always has the right number of players, and they make the crew less certain of each other.
- **Evil only knows each other at 7+.** In small games, the Saboteur doesn't know who the Parasite is and the Parasite gets no bluffs. That's important for balance with few players.
- **Roles unlock with size.** Small games use simple, high-information roles. The Smuggler (+2 Drifters) is held back until 7+ so tiny games aren't drowned in Drifters. Roles like the Gunner and First Officer need 5+, because at 3 players a single lucky shot (or "3 alive, no airlock") would end the game instantly.
- **ARIA deals balanced sets.** At least half the Crew are always information roles, so the good team is never blind.

### Short Haul (3–4 players)
BotC officially starts at 5, so 3–4 players is our quick mode, about 10 minutes:
- No Saboteurs, no Drifters, and the Scanner has no ghost signal (too confusing with so few players).
- With 3 players the Parasite waits until **night 3** to kill. That gives the two crew members two nights of information before they must guess.
- It plays more like a tense guessing game than a full Clocktower game. Use it to learn the rules, then play with 5+.

### Game length
Day timers scale with the number of living players, so games feel like BotC: long discussions early, shorter as the circle shrinks. These are robot playtests at "Standard" pace; human games run a bit longer because people talk more.

| Players | Typical length |
|---|---|
| 3–4 | ~10 min |
| 5 | ~15–20 min |
| 7 | ~25–30 min |
| 10 | ~45–60 min |
| 12 | ~60 min |
| 15 | ~90 min |

The host or Captain can pick **Relaxed (×1.3)**, **Standard** or **Quick (×0.7)**. The Captain can also add or remove time and pause at any moment.

### Balance knobs (for the Captain)
These are the same tools a BotC Storyteller uses to keep games close:
1. **Hand-pick roles** in the lobby, checked against the setup table.
2. **Edit night information** before dawn, especially the false info glitched or drunk players see.
3. **Registration:** choose whether the Stowaway shows up as evil and the Mimic as good.
4. **Window clues:** show a clue for the side that's behind.
5. **Save the First Officer** (bounce the kill) or add or remove a death.

## 4. A round

### 🌙 Night
Everyone "sleeps" and the 3D ship goes dark. In this order:
1. *(Night 1, 7+ players)* Saboteurs learn who the Parasite is; the Parasite learns its Saboteurs and **3 bluffs** (good roles not in play).
2. Hacker glitches → (night 1) Comms Officer, Archivist, Security Chief, Navigator get their info → Medic shields → **Parasite kills** → Black Box wakes if killed → Coroner → Engineer → Scanner → Service Droid picks a master → Mimic sees the Manifest.

Players with an ability choose targets on their screen. If they don't choose in time, ARIA chooses for them.

**Nobody is bored at night.** Everyone gets a **drawing pad**. Pictures appear on easels around the ship the next morning (anonymous unless you sign them). Infiltrators who know each other also get a **secret night chat**.

### ☀️ Day
| Part | What happens | Default length |
|---|---|---|
| **Dawn** | The Captain's (or ARIA's) story of the night appears in a **cloud bubble**; deaths play their **death animations** at the bridge table | ~10 s + 4 s per death |
| **Explore** | Walk the ship. **Proximity chat** (text and voice): only players in the same room or nearby hear you, which recreates BotC's private conversations. Do **tasks**, look for **clues**, find the **drawings** | 25 s per living player (+90 s on day 1) |
| **Emergency meeting** | Everyone's pulled back to their seats on the bridge. Open discussion | 12 s per living player |
| **Nominations** | Click a player on the bridge table to nominate them. The **nominator accuses (20 s)**, the **nominee defends (20 s)**, then the **vote clock** sweeps clockwise from the seat after the nominee | 30 s per living player |
| **Dusk** | The player "on the block" is **airlocked** | ~9 s |

### Nominations & voting (exactly like BotC)
- Each *living* player may nominate **once per day**; each player may be nominated **once per day**.
- Raise your hand before the clock hand reaches your seat. When it passes you, your vote is **locked**.
- A nominee goes "on the block" with votes **≥ half the living players** (rounded up) **and** more votes than anyone else that day. **A tie means nobody** goes on the block, and the next nominee must beat the tied number.
- One airlocking per day at most.
- **Dead players** stay at the table as ghosts. They can talk and get **one ghost vote** for the rest of the game.

## 5. Lies, glitches and registration
- **Glitched** (Hacker) and **drunk** (Space Drunk) players receive **false information**. ARIA writes believable false info automatically; the Captain can rewrite it.
- **Stowaway** may register as evil (even as the Parasite) to the Scanner, Engineer, Navigator, Security Chief, Coroner, Black Box and Gunner.
- **Mimic** may register as good and as a Crew or Drifter role.
- **Ghost signal:** one good player always pings the Scanner as the Parasite.

## 6. The Captain & the Command Station
Whoever creates the ship chooses one of:
- **🤖 Autopilot:** everyone plays; **ARIA** runs the timers, writes the stories and makes every Storyteller decision.
- **👨‍✈️ Captain:** one person runs the game and doesn't play, just like a BotC Storyteller. If the Captain disconnects for 90 s mid-game, ARIA takes over.

The Command Station has six tabs:
| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| ▶ **Control** | Next phase, +1 min / ±30 s, pause, auto-advance, who has made their night choice |
| 📒 **Manifest** | The grimoire: every role, who's glitched, the ghost signal, Droid masters, ghost votes, recent notes, the Parasite's bluffs; kill or revive anyone |
| 🌙 **Night** | Everything that happened tonight; **edit every message** (false ones are flagged); add or remove deaths; pick each **death animation**; write **tonight's story** |
| ☁️ **Story** | Speak in a **cloud bubble** any time, quick joke announcements, and **puppet any player**: dance, faint, chicken, moonwalk, levitate, sneeze, confetti, zap, grow, shrink… |
| 🔭 **Clues & art** | Force a window clue, set Stowaway/Mimic registration, remove drawings |
| ⚠️ **Danger** | Hand over to ARIA, end the game for either team, **reset everything** |

**Death animations:** airlocked · consumed by tentacles · spaghettified · abducted by UFO · melted · confetti explosion · frozen solid · turned into a rubber duck · floated away · fainted dramatically · shot.

## 7. Tasks, the Observation Array and window clues
There are 11 rooms, each with a task console and a quick minigame:

| Room | Task |
|---|---|
| Engine Room | Calibrate the Thrusters (timing) |
| Reactor | Stabilise the Core (memory sequence) |
| Medbay | Analyse Alien Samples (matching pairs) |
| Hydroponics | Water the Moon Plants |
| Comms | Tune the Distress Signal (match the wave) |
| Navigation | Plot an Escape Course (connect the stars) |
| Galley | Cook Space Noodles (follow the recipe) |
| Cargo Bay | Catch the Escaped Hamsters (whack-a-mole) |
| Observation Deck | Align the Telescope |
| Airlock | Purge the Vents (button mash) |
| Crew Quarters | Feed the Ship Cat (it dodges) |

- Each task (once per player per day, ghosts too) charges the **Observation Array**. When it reaches 1.5 × the number of living players, a **clue** appears outside the **Observation Deck window** the next morning.
- **Clues help whichever team is losing.** ARIA compares how close the ship is to the black hole (good losing) against how many evil players are dead or under suspicion (evil losing).

| If the crew is behind | If the infiltrators are behind |
|---|---|
| 💀 A *dead constellation* in the shape of a good role that is **not** aboard (often one of the Parasite's bluffs) | ✨ A *living constellation* of a Crew role that **is** aboard |
| ☄️ Three comets in three players' suit colours: **one carries spores** (is evil) | ☄️ Three comets crossing a constellation: one of them has that role |
| 🛰️ A derelict probe blinking one player's colour: **not** the Parasite | 💡 Debris lights blink the number of Drifters |

Clues are visual and cryptic. Nobody is told who the clue helps; you have to go to the window and decode it.

**Social tell:** a team that's winning may *avoid* doing tasks, and that can give them away.

## 8. The ship
- **3D, Among Us-style** top-down view (Three.js), with 12 rooms joined by corridors, collisions, a minimap and the round bridge table with a seat for every player.
- **Spooky atmosphere:** the black hole grows outside every window and beneath the ship as players die. Lights dim and flicker more the closer it gets. The ambient drone changes with the phase (calm, uneasy, night whispers, a heartbeat during nominations).
- **Everyone looks the same** (a spacesuit) for the whole game. Customise your **colour**, **hat**, **visor tint** and **pet**.
- **Sounds:** every effect is synthesised in the browser, with no audio files: airlock whoosh, gavel, vote clock ticks, task chimes, death stings, the dawn chime.
- **Voice:** optional proximity voice chat (WebRTC). Volume fades with distance while exploring, everyone is at full volume in meetings, and mics are muted at night.

## 9. The epic reveal
When the game ends, ARIA plays back the ship's black box:
1. **THE TRUTH:** everyone stands on a stage in front of the black hole.
2. **Unmasking:** one by one, each suit bursts off to show their **hidden character model**, one per role: the Engineer's hard hat and wrench, the Hacker's glowing laptop, the Stowaway peeking out of a cardboard box, the Parasite as a many-eyed tentacled horror… The Space Drunk gets a special "you thought you were the Medic!" moment.
3. **Night-by-night replay:** glowing beams show every hack, shield, kill, scan and Black Box check, with captions saying whether each piece of information was TRUE or FALSE. The Captain's stories come back in cloud bubbles.
4. **Day replays:** nominations, Gunner shots, Sentinel zaps and airlockings.
5. **Finale:** *"THE CREW ESCAPES!"* (the black hole collapses, confetti, the crew bounces) or *"NO MORE SPACE"* (the black hole swallows the stage, red lights, the Parasite grows huge).
6. **Summary** of every role, then **Watch again** or **Back to the docking bay**.
